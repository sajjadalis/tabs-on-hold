# Get started

Tabs on Hold saves tabs into sessions, with screenshot previews to help you find them again.

Select a screenshot in this guide to view it full size.

Install Tabs on Hold from the [Chrome Web Store](#).

## Pin the extension

Open Chrome’s **Extensions** menu and pin **Tabs on Hold**. Click its toolbar icon to choose which tabs to save.

Chrome 116 or later is required.

## Save your tabs

1. Click **Tabs on Hold** in the toolbar.
2. Choose the current tab, selected tabs, the current window, or all regular windows.
3. Leave **Capture visible-area previews** enabled if you want screenshots.
4. Click **Set tabs aside**.

Chrome asks for optional website access when needed for previews. Choose **Save without previews** to save URLs without granting that access.

Tabs close after their data is saved. Failed captures leave their source tabs open. Internal pages and incognito tabs are excluded. Pinned tabs are excluded unless you enable them in Settings.

## Open your saved library

Click the saved tab/session count at the bottom of the popup, or press `Alt+Shift+S`.

Use **Sessions** to browse by session, **All tabs** for the whole library, or **Favorites** for pages you marked.

[![Saved sessions with real website previews and sessions containing 3, 4, and 5 tabs](/screenshots/sessions.png)](/screenshots/sessions.png)

## Organize your library

- Click a session’s name to rename it.
- Drag tabs to reorder them or move them between sessions.
- Use a tab’s menu to move it without dragging.
- Click a star to favorite a tab or session.

Use the arrow beside a session to collapse or expand it. Select multiple tabs to move, restore, or delete them together.

### Find a saved tab

Search by page title, website, or session name. Press `/` to focus search. In this example, searching for “design” finds the five tabs in **Design & product**.

Use filters and sorting to narrow the results further.

[![Searching for design returns five saved tabs from the Design & product session](/screenshots/search.png)](/screenshots/search.png)

### Switch to list view

Use the list button above the library for a compact view with page titles, websites, and preview and restore actions. Switch back to card view to browse by screenshot.

[![List view in dark appearance, with session counts and preview and restore controls](/screenshots/list-dark.png)](/screenshots/list-dark.png)

## Preview a saved page

Click a screenshot or choose **Preview** to open its saved visible area without reopening the website. Choose **Done** to return to your library, or **Restore tab** to open that page.

Previews show the visible area at capture time, rather than the whole scrolling page. They are snapshots, so a website may look different when you reopen it.

[![A saved Stripe screenshot open in the preview dialog, with Done and Restore tab buttons](/screenshots/preview.png)](/screenshots/preview.png)

## Restore your tabs

- Choose **Restore** for one saved tab.
- Use a session’s **Restore** button to open the whole session in a new window.
- Choose **Open without removing** from a tab’s menu to open it and keep the saved copy.

**Remove after restoring** is enabled by default. Turn it off to keep restored pages in your library. A failed restore keeps its saved data.

Saved pinned state, muted state, and tab groups are restored. Unsaved forms, page history, scroll position, and playback state are not saved. A session does not recreate the original arrangement of browser windows.

## Choose your appearance

Use the sun or moon button at the top of the library to switch between light and dark. In **Settings**, choose **System** to follow your computer’s appearance.

[![All twelve saved tabs displayed as cards in dark appearance](/screenshots/library-dark.png)](/screenshots/library-dark.png)

## Keep a backup

Choose **Export a backup** in the saved library sidebar to export a JSON copy of your sessions and screenshots. See [Backups & imports](backups.md) for steps.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut      | Action                          |
| ------------- | ------------------------------- |
| `Alt+Shift+A` | Save tabs in the current window |
| `Alt+Shift+S` | Open the saved library          |
| `/`           | Focus search in the library     |

Change Chrome shortcuts at `chrome://extensions/shortcuts`.
